02 / ABOUTARIZONA

ABOUT
GINTER *

Jialong Xu (Ginter) is a media artist and photographer based in Arizona.

His work explores the intersection of generative visuals, immersive environments, and audiovisual performance.

Using tools such as TouchDesigner, he creates visual experiences where sound, motion, and interaction shape evolving digital landscapes.

Alongside his artistic practice, he runs GINTER STUDIO, producing photography and multimedia work across portrait, automotive, and experimental visual projects.
